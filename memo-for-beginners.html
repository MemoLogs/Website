Memo for Beginners

Someone hears your radio ad, searches for your business and buys. Which part of the plan helped make that sale happen?

See a radio example ↓

How much did radio add?

A search ad may get credit for the last click. That is attribution. To see what radio added, compare sales in similar places with different radio schedules. That is incrementality.

The estimate assumes the markets would have followed similar trends. Check delivery, promotions, seasonality and the range around the estimate. The advertiser can then weigh the profit on the extra orders against the cost of the spots.

What does an AI buying agent do?

An agent can read a media plan, propose a change and carry it out within the buyer’s rules. For example, an advertiser can approve a budget increase on a connected ad account. MemoLogs checks the spend limit, applies the change and checks the account again.

Buying platforms can also bid for ad space in real time. The platform, account permissions and inventory determine which purchases can go through.

How do customers find you?

SEO

Search engine optimization helps customers find your pages in regular search results. Compare the terms people search for, which brands rank and where each result leads.

AEO

Answer engine optimization focuses on how AI answers describe your business. Ask a fixed set of customer questions, save the answers and see which brands and sources appear.

Both checks can guide a category review. Connect any change in visibility to visits, leads and sales before treating it as a business result.
