01 Why we exist

Marketing's tools got great at measuring.
They never learned to remember.

A modern marketing team runs more instrumentation than a mid-size bank — and still argues about what worked. Three failures compound underneath, and every one of them charges you twice: once when it happens, and again when it happens next year.

01 — INFLATED

The number is wrong before you use it.

Platforms grade their own homework, so reported conversions arrive structurally over-counted. Every downstream budget call inherits that error — you aren't optimizing, you're calibrating on fiction.

02 — LATE

The proof arrives after it matters.

Traditional media-mix modeling explains last quarter to this quarter's meeting. By readout day the market has moved twice, and the answer has already expired.

03 — FORGOTTEN

The learning walks out the door.

The reasoning behind a call lives in a deck, a thread, and one person's head. When that person leaves, the organization pays full price to discover the same thing again.

Our thesis

Measurement without memory is a treadmill. Prove what actually worked, keep the reasoning attached to the result, and every quarter should start smarter than the last one — that is the product.

03 Principles

Five convictions we build against.

These aren't values on a wall. Each one shows up as something the product refuses to do.

What we hold to

  1. Correlation is not a budget decision.

    Every recommendation traces back to an experiment or a causal model — never to a last-click credit a platform assigned itself. If we can't show the counterfactual, we don't call it lift.

  2. A system that can't be wrong can't be trusted.

    Every verdict carries a trust state, and the platform declines when the data can't defend a conclusion. A confident answer on thin evidence is the most expensive thing software can hand a marketer.

  3. The decision is the asset — not the dashboard.

    We record what was decided, what it was based on, and how it turned out. Charts describe the past; a decision record is what lets the next person inherit judgment instead of re-deriving it.

  4. Our incentives shouldn't move your budget.

    Flat fee, never a percentage of media spend. We get paid identically whether the honest answer is to scale a channel or to shut it off — which is the only way “spend less here” stays a sentence we can say.

  5. Your data trains your memory, not our model.

    Customer integration data is not used to train generalized AI or machine-learning models unless a customer separately agrees to it. What your account teaches the system stays yours.

04 How we operate

Easier to judge us by what we won't do.

Most of what shapes a platform is decided by what it rules out. Here are both columns, plainly.

✓ What we do
  • Wire the integrations ourselves. API-first, no rip-and-replace, no engineering sprint on your side.
  • Prove it on your own account. A 90-day pilot with a causal verdict inside five weeks, not a twelve-week readout.
  • Show our work. Source-cited answers, per-channel measured-vs-claimed lift, and the assumptions behind both.
  • Price flat. One fee, no seat licenses, no percentage of spend.
  • Stay reversible. Rollback anytime in the first 30 days, and GDPR-ready from day one.
✕ What we won't do
  • Take a cut of your media spend. It quietly pays us to tell you to spend more.
  • Build or sell an identity graph. Audience work is done causally, without one.
  • Train generalized models on your data. Not without your separate, explicit agreement.
  • Ship a number the model can't defend. We would rather return “not enough signal” than a confident guess.
  • Sell a dashboard and call it a decision. If it doesn't change what you do next, it isn't the product.

Know what actually works. Every campaign. Every dollar.
