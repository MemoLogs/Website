Product · Demo · Why now · Wedge · Business model · Ask):

3. The path: proposal → policy decision → approval → execution → verification → outcome
4. A held breath, one line addressed to the visitor

7. For finance and for investors: wedge, launch promise, pricing principle, the proof the next financing needs

## 4. Feeling curve and the peak

```
1  Unease → order   five actors drifting loose in 3D; scroll pulls them into one hub and the rods grow
2  Recognition      the five places one $140K decision lived, named plainly, in a wiped-in list
3  Clarity          six stations pass sideways, one label each, a pipeline read as breadth
4  Anticipation     an almost empty screen and one line in second person (AUTHORED SILENCE, act 4)

6  Inevitability    a wipe changes the ground: agents enter at rung three of the ladder
7  Confidence       what launches, who it is for, how it is paid, what proof looks like
8  Resolve          the mark returns, the last rod connects, the record seals and refuses an edit
```

**Peak (act 5), as a visitor would say it:** "It asked to move $140K, the page
checked the evidence, found it was 134 days old, cut the move down to a $60K
test and wrote the reason in front of me."

**Authored silence:** act 4 is deliberately near-empty (one line, ground only)
so act 5 has something to arrive from. The verification pass should read it as
anticipation, not dead scroll.

## 5. The one thing no site does (signature move seed)

Authored: the page builds the product's core object, a Prospective Decision
Record, as you scroll: proposal, expectation, policy version, evidence state,
authorization result, approver, execution receipt, outcome. At the close the
record seals, and when you try to edit a field it does not change; a correction
event appends underneath with the original preserved. The product's invariant
("corrections append events; nothing rewrites history") made tactile.

## Grammar

**Filmic one-shot.** Why the other seven lost:

- Chaptered editorial: forbids media above the fold and the pinned demo; the pitch has to land on screen one for an investor.
- Live surface: forbids the hero claim and display type; the audience needs the argument, not only the tool. (Its honesty rule is kept anyway: the demo computes from data in the page and is labelled a demo scenario.)
- Continuous world: no geography to travel; Ritvik chose distinct scenes; the most fragile build for a two-still budget.
- Typographic poster: the brand's asset is a 3D mark and the request is a 3D site.
- Gallery/catalog: nothing here is a range of objects.
- Split stage: the comparison (fragmented vs governed) is one act, not the whole page, and the grammar bans the layered hero.
- Rhythmic cutlist: wrong pulse for a governance product sold to a CFO.

## Signature move

**The record that only appends.** A fixed Decision Record panel (right edge on
desktop, a one-line strip on phones) appears after the hero and fills field by
field as acts pass, using the demo scenario's values computed in the page. On
the close stage it seals with a trusted-timestamp line. Clicking a value opens
an inline input; submitting appends a `correction` event under the record with
"original preserved" and the value stays. Coded in the page off `--sc-p` and
its own `data-ml-*` attributes; engine untouched.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where you watch a $140K budget move get checked, cut down to a

beside you and then refuses to be edited.

## Score

| Act | Beat | Device | Span (vh) | Why |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Unease → order | `pin` + live WebGL subject + `parallax` far/foreground planes + pointer tilt | 2.3 | Scattered becomes ordered under the reader's hand; the strongest open the mark itself can give |
| 2 | Recognition | `flow` + `in` + `reveal` | natural | Five places, named. Reads as a document because it is one |
| 3 | Clarity | `pan` | 3.2 | Lateral travel reads as a pipeline |
| 4 | Anticipation | `flow` (silence) | natural, short | Empty ground before the peak |
| 5 | Tension → relief (PEAK) | `pin` + bespoke record logic | 3.8 | Largest span on the page; the argument is the demo |
| 6 | Inevitability | `reveal` (full-bleed wipe) + `in` | 1.4 | A wipe is a change of state: agents arrive |
| 7 | Confidence | `flow` + `in` (asymmetric two-column) | natural | Administrative content, compressed |
| 8 | Resolve | `pin` + WebGL returns + the sealed record + `magnet` CTA | 1.3 | The page stops moving and starts responding |

Families: pin, flow, pan, reveal, parallax, pointer (tilt/magnet). No family
twice in a row. Zero scrub acts (no clip budget). Total pinned/pan span ≈ 12vh
plus natural flow sections; eight acts, outside the 6-7 act / 13.6-13.8vh band.

## Language rules carried from the doc

"Every covered high-consequence action", never "every dollar". "Independently
sourced" outcome, never "truth". "Tamper-evident record created on the
authorization path". No invented statistics: the only numbers on the page are
inside the labelled demo scenario and the doc's own policy defaults.
